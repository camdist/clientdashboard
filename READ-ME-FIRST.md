# Accounts & Metrics update v3.5

Extract this ZIP. Upload its folders/files to the root of camdist/clientdashboard, retaining paths and replacing matching files. Commit and let Cloudflare build; refresh the dashboard afterward.

Read ACCOUNTS-v3.5.md. Open Accounts & pages to add any platform and follow its simple guide. Choose the correct account in content details, add the published post link, mark Published, save and click Sync metrics.

Registering an account alone does not grant API access. Six adapters provide selected metrics after one-time platform authorization and per-account Cloudflare secret setup. Other platforms support registration and post links. One-click OAuth, scheduled sync and token renewal are not included.

This is cumulative, including the Themes, Content Type and stylesheet fixes. No migration or new dependency is required. wrangler.jsonc, config/app.json, passwords and credentials are excluded.

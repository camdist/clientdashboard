# Your Client Dashboard — First 10 minutes

Your seller or setup helper must deploy the dashboard and give you its private website address and login first. The desktop download is a shortcut to that website, so internet access is required.

1. **Make it yours.** Customize → enter your business name, logo, timezone and currency. Choose a Theme. Turn off sections you do not use.
2. **Add a client.** Clients → add name, logo and contact details. Use the client's profile to keep their work together.
3. **Plan work once.** Add projects and tasks. Link a task to its project or content record instead of copying details into multiple lists.
4. **Plan a month.** Calendar → download the Excel template, fill your plan, upload it and check the preview. Choose Agenda for an easier small-screen list.
5. **Track content.** Content Type → All, Short-form, Long-form or your custom format. Edit one content record to update calendar, workflow and analytics together.
6. **Add useful links/files.** Resources stores reusable documents. Attach them to related content.
7. **Create invoices.** Payments → add an invoice and line items. Numbers and totals are calculated; download the PDF. Record payments you received. This does not charge customers, send invoices automatically or perform bookkeeping/tax filing.
8. **Get post metrics the easy way.** Add an account in Accounts & pages. Link published posts to it. Export a post-level CSV from your reporting tool, then Analytics → Import analytics CSV. Choose the account, map columns, check matches and import. See RELEASE-v3.6.md for details.
9. **Read charts correctly.** Choose a graph and metric. The month is the month posts were published. Reporting basis separates lifetime totals from dated reporting windows.
10. **Protect your work.** Backup & restore → Download full backup regularly. Deleted items go to Trash. Existing records are kept when you restore a backup; use a separate fresh deployment to roll back edits.

Store backups privately. Your business owner login grants access to the whole workspace; it is not a restricted login for each client. Ask your seller/setup helper to change the password or handle hosting issues. Never paste API tokens into content, account fields, messages or GitHub.

Before using real work, create a test client, invoice, post and file. Check a download, CSV import, Trash restore and full backup with your setup helper.
